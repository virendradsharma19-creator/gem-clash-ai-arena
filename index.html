💎 How to Play

Move rule: drag a gem only one space left, right, up or down onto a neighbouring tile. Diagonal moves and dragging across multiple tiles are not allowed. You can also tap two adjacent gems.

Match three or more gems of the same colour to attack and build ultimate energy. You have 10 seconds per move; when time expires, the AI takes its turn.

  • Choose your setup: select a champion, arena, rival, difficulty and battle mode.
  • Special gems: Bombs and Lightning activate when swapped one tile with a neighbour. Prism clears the colour of the gem you swap it with. Matching four in a line creates Lightning; matching five creates Prism; T/L matches create Bombs.
  • Hero Skills: spend 40 energy for a champion-specific skill: Arcane Pulse (Mage), Shield Bash (Knight), Star Volley (Ranger), or Flame Burst (Phoenix).
  • Ultimate: spend 100 energy for your selected hero’s signature ultimate attack.
  • Shield Burst: spend 60 energy to recover health.
  • Hero collection & mastery: unlock champions with coins, then train each hero up to Mastery Level 5 for +5% attack and +5 max health per level. Each hero has separate mastery progress.
  • Earn rewards: wins give coins and XP. Spend coins on champions and upgrades.
  • Campaign story: choose an unlocked stage to read its story and mission objective before starting. Clear stages to continue the story.
  • Boss battles: stages 3, 6 and 9 have extra health. Watch for a warning every third boss turn—special attacks deal heavy damage.
  • Progress: saved in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data may erase it.

This is a local browser game with scripted computer opponents. Online multiplayer and a native Android/iOS app are not included in this build.
